Terms and cancellation policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

These terms apply when you buy a CV, cover letter or LinkedIn profile service from Ink & Offer for yourself, as a consumer. Please read them before you order, especially section 6 (your right to cancel), section 8 (what we won't do) and section 9 (no guarantee of interviews or jobs). You can save or print this page for your records.

Career coaches and other businesses who use our white-label service are covered by our separate partner terms, not these terms.

1. About us

Ink & Offer is a CV, cover letter and LinkedIn profile writing service based in the United Kingdom.

  • Email: hello@inkandoffer.com

To contact us about anything, including to cancel or complain, email hello@inkandoffer.com. We will reply within 2 working days.

2. Our services

We offer:

  • One application: a CV (or résumé) and a cover letter, rewritten for one job advert that you choose.
  • Extra applications: a further CV and cover letter for a different job advert, at the extra-application price, if you order within 90 days of your first order.
  • LinkedIn profile rewrite: new text for your LinkedIn headline, About section and experience entries. We send you the text to paste into your profile. We never ask for your LinkedIn password and never log in to your account.
  • Any other service we describe on our website or in a written quote.

We write to the conventions of the country you choose: UK, Irish, Australian or New Zealand CV, or US résumé, with the matching spelling. You receive editable Word (.docx) and PDF files, or plain text for a LinkedIn profile.

Our documents are based only on the information you give us. A person reviews every line of every document against that information before we deliver it.

3. Prices and payment

  • The price is the one shown on our website, or in our written quote, when you order. It is the total you pay. We are not registered for VAT or for any overseas sales tax, so none is added.
  • Prices are shown in pounds sterling (GBP), euros (EUR), US dollars (USD), Australian dollars (AUD) and New Zealand dollars (NZD). You pay in the currency of the price you chose. Your card provider may charge a currency conversion fee; that is outside our control.
  • You pay in full, in advance, by card through a secure Stripe payment link. We never see or store your full card details.
  • We will send you a confirmation by email after you pay, setting out what you ordered, the price, your choice about when we start, and the time we will deliver.
  • If we have made an obvious mistake in a price, we will tell you before we start work. You can then go ahead at the correct price or cancel for a full refund.

4. How an order works

  1. You send us your current CV (or your full work history and education) and the job advert, or for a LinkedIn rewrite, your CV or current profile text. Tell us anything you want emphasised or left out.
  2. We check we have what we need and confirm the price.
  3. You pay using our Stripe payment link. At checkout you choose whether you want us to start straight away (see section 6) and agree to these terms.
  4. We write and review your documents.
  5. We deliver them by email to the address you ordered from.

Our contract with you starts when your payment is confirmed. We may decline an order for any reason before we start work. If we do, we will tell you promptly and refund anything you have paid in full.

You must be 18 or over to order.

5. Turnaround and the late-delivery promise

Standard turnaround is 12 hours. The 12 hours run in clock hours, including weekends, and start when all of the following have happened:

  • we have received your CV and the job advert (and anything else we have told you we need);
  • your payment is confirmed; and
  • you have asked us to start within the cancellation period (see section 6). If you have not asked, we start when your cancellation period ends.

If those things are all in place between 8pm and 8am UK time, the 12 hours start at 8am UK time. We will confirm your delivery time by email, in your time zone.

If we deliver more than 12 hours after the delivery time we confirmed, that order is free: we will refund the full price, even though you keep the documents.

This promise does not apply where the delay is caused by:

  • you, for example if you send information late, change the job advert or your instructions after we start, or we are waiting for your answer to a question we need answered; or
  • events outside our reasonable control, such as a serious illness or a major outage at one of our providers. If this happens, we will tell you as soon as we can, give you a new delivery time, and you can cancel for a full refund if you no longer want the order.

If we expect to be late for any reason, we will tell you as soon as we know.

6. Your right to cancel

You have the legal right to cancel your order within 14 days without giving any reason. This comes from the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013.

The cancellation period ends 14 days after the day our contract is made, which is the day your payment is confirmed.

Asking us to start straight away. Because our turnaround is 12 hours, most customers want us to start straight away. The law says we cannot start work within your 14-day cancellation period unless you expressly ask us to. At checkout, you can tick a box to ask us to start now. If you do not tick it, we will wait until the cancellation period ends before we start, and your 12-hour turnaround will start then.

If you ask us to start straight away, you acknowledge that:

  • you will lose your right to cancel once we have fully performed the service, which is when we deliver your completed documents; and
  • if you cancel after we have started but before we deliver, you will pay for the work done up to the time you tell us you are cancelling, in proportion to the full service. We will refund the rest.

The free revision in section 7 is an extra we offer after delivery. Asking for one does not restart or extend your right to cancel.

How to cancel. Tell us clearly that you want to cancel, by emailing hello@inkandoffer.com. You can use the model cancellation form below, but you don't have to. To meet the deadline, you just need to send your message before the cancellation period ends. We will confirm that we have received it.

Your refund. If you cancel within the cancellation period, we will refund what you have paid, minus any amount for work done at your request, without undue delay and no later than 14 days after the day you told us. We will refund you using the same card you paid with, and we will not charge you a fee for the refund.

Each order is a separate contract. For example, cancelling an extra application does not affect an order we have already delivered.

7. Revisions

One free revision is included with every order, if you ask for it within 7 days of delivery.

A revision covers:

  • correcting errors or anything we got wrong about your experience;
  • changes to wording, emphasis, order or length; and
  • anything that reflects the instructions you gave us when you ordered.

We will deliver your revision within 12 hours of receiving a clear revision request, on the same clock as section 5.

A revision does not cover a different job, employer or country format, or substantial new material such as a role that was not in the information you first sent. We can quote for these as a new order. We will usually fix small typing errors free of charge even after your revision is used.

8. What we won't do

We will not:

  • invent or exaggerate qualifications, employers, job titles, dates, results, skills or experience, or include anything we know or suspect to be false or misleading;
  • add anything that is not supported by the information you give us;
  • complete application forms, tests, visa, immigration, academic or security-vetting forms for you, or apply for jobs on your behalf;
  • contact employers or recruiters for you, or log in to any of your accounts; or
  • give legal, immigration, tax, financial or recruitment advice.

If your instructions would need us to do any of these things, we will tell you and leave it out.

9. No guarantee of interviews or jobs

We will write your documents with reasonable care and skill, to help you present your real experience as strongly and clearly as possible for the role you chose. But hiring decisions depend on many things outside our control, such as the number of applicants, the employer's requirements and your interview. We do not guarantee that you will get an interview, a job offer or any particular salary.

10. Your responsibilities

You agree to:

  • give us information that is true, accurate and complete, and tell us promptly if anything is wrong;
  • check every document before you use it, and tell us about any error. You are responsible for the final content of any document you send to an employer;
  • only send information about other people, such as referees, if you need to and you are entitled to share it; and
  • remove information we don't need, such as health information, your date of birth, photographs and identity numbers. Our privacy notice explains how we handle it if you send it anyway.

11. Your rights if something goes wrong

The Consumer Rights Act 2015 says we must provide our services with reasonable care and skill, and that anything we tell you about the service which you rely on is binding.

If we don't, you can ask us to put it right by redoing the relevant part of the service at no cost to you, within a reasonable time. If we can't put it right, or don't do so within a reasonable time and without significant inconvenience to you, you can ask for a price reduction, which in some cases may be a full refund. These legal rights are in addition to our free revision and our late-delivery promise, and nothing in these terms affects them. For more information, visit Citizens Advice at citizensadvice.org.uk.

Once you have paid, you own the copyright in the finished documents we write for you and can use, edit and share them as you like. We keep ownership of our own templates, methods and know-how.

14. Your personal information

We use your personal information as described in our privacy notice. In short: we use it only to provide your order, and we delete your CV, the job advert and your documents 30 days after we deliver your final documents.

If we break these terms, we are responsible for loss or damage you suffer that is a foreseeable result of our breach or our failure to use reasonable care and skill. Loss or damage is foreseeable if it is obvious that it will happen, or if we both knew it might happen when you placed your order.

We are not responsible for:

  • the outcome of any job application, or any employer's or recruiter's decision;
  • errors that come from inaccurate or incomplete information you gave us, or from changes you or someone else make to the documents after delivery; or

Our services are for your personal use in applying for jobs. Nothing in these terms limits or excludes our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, for breach of your legal rights in relation to the service, or for anything else that the law does not allow us to limit or exclude.

16. Complaints

If you are unhappy with our service, please email hello@inkandoffer.com with your order details and what went wrong. We will acknowledge your complaint within 2 working days and aim to give you a full response within 14 days. If we cannot resolve your complaint, we will let you know; we do not currently use an alternative dispute resolution provider. You can also get free advice from Citizens Advice or, outside the UK, your local consumer organisation, and you can go to court.

We may update these terms from time to time. The terms that apply to your order are the ones on our website when you placed it.

  • You may not transfer your rights under these terms to someone else without our agreement.
  • No one else has any rights under these terms.
  • If a court finds part of these terms unlawful, the rest will continue in force.
  • If we don't enforce our rights straight away, we can still enforce them later.

19. Governing law and where you can bring a claim

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales. You can bring a claim in the courts of England and Wales. If you live in Scotland or Northern Ireland, you can also bring a claim in your local courts, and Scots or Northern Irish law will apply to the extent the law requires.

If you live outside the UK, for example in Ireland, the United States, Australia or New Zealand, you keep any protection given to you by the mandatory consumer laws of the country where you live, and you may be able to bring a claim in your local courts. Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have under those laws.
