How it works

From brief to finished files in four quiet steps.

  1. 1

    Send the brief

    The client's current CV, the job advert, and anything to emphasise or avoid.

  2. 2

    We tailor

    The CV is rewritten around that advert, in the right format for the country (UK/Irish CV, US résumé, AU/NZ CV), with a cover letter to match.

  3. 3

    Line-by-line review

    Every claim is checked against the source CV before anything leaves us.

  4. 4

    You deliver

    Unbranded Word and PDF files within 12 hours, ready for your branding. One revision included.

What to send us

Everything we need for a strong brief.

  • The client's current CV (Word or PDF)
  • The job advert or link
  • Target country
  • Anything to emphasise or avoid (gaps, sensitive history)
  • Your house template, if you have one
