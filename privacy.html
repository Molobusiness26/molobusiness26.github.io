Privacy notice

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This notice explains what personal information Ink & Offer collects when you use our CV, cover letter and LinkedIn profile writing services or our website, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and your rights. We have written it in plain English. If anything is unclear, email us at hello@inkandoffer.com.

1. Who we are

Ink & Offer is a CV, cover letter and LinkedIn profile writing service ("Ink & Offer", "we", "us").

  • Email: hello@inkandoffer.com

When you order from us directly, we are the controller of your personal information under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. That means we decide how and why it is used, and we are responsible for looking after it.

We are a small business and have not appointed a data protection officer. Please send any privacy question to the email address above.

2. If a career coach ordered for you

Some career coaches and outplacement firms use us to write documents that they deliver to their own clients under their own brand. If your coach ordered your documents, your coach is the controller and we act only as their processor: we use your information only on their instructions, only to write your documents, and we never contact you directly.

In that case, your coach's own privacy notice applies, and you should contact your coach about your information. If you contact us, we will pass your request to your coach promptly. The rest of this notice applies to people who order from us directly and to visitors to our website. Our commitments on security and on deleting files 30 days after delivery apply to coaches' clients too.

3. The information we collect

Information you give us when you order

  • Your name, email address and, if you give it, your phone number and the country you live in.
  • Your current CV or résumé, and anything else you send us to work from, such as an old cover letter, your LinkedIn profile text or notes about your achievements.
  • The job advert you are applying for, and the job title and employer.
  • Your instructions: what to emphasise, what to leave out, the tone you want and the format (for example, a UK CV or a US résumé).
  • Your choices at checkout, including whether you asked us to start work within your 14-day cancellation period, and your agreement to our terms.

Information created while we work

  • The draft and final CV, cover letter or LinkedIn profile text we write for you.
  • Emails between us, including revision requests and any complaint.

Payment information

  • We take payment through Stripe. We never see or store your full card details. Stripe tells us your name, email address, the amount, the date, the last four digits and type of your card, and whether the payment succeeded.

Information about other people

  • Your CV may name other people, such as referees or former managers. Please only include them if you need to, and tell them you are sharing their details. We recommend leaving referees off the CV you send us; we do not need them to write your documents.

Information collected when you visit our website

  • We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on our website, and we do not have access to visitor logs. Our website host and our font provider do receive some technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser loads our pages. See section 10.

4. Information we don't need, and what happens if you send it

We do not need, and ask you to remove before you send your CV:

  • health information, including disability or medical history;
  • your date of birth or age;
  • photographs of you;
  • National Insurance, Social Security, passport or other identity numbers, and bank details;
  • other sensitive information, such as your religion, sexual orientation, political views, trade union membership or criminal record.

Some of these are "special category" information under the UK GDPR and need extra protection.

If you send it anyway, we will not use it to write your documents and we will leave it out of the documents we write, unless you clearly ask us to include something (for example, an explanation of a career break). If you do ask, we will rely on your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time before we deliver by emailing us. Either way, anything you sent is deleted with the rest of your order files, 30 days after delivery, or sooner if you ask.

Take payment and issue receipts Name, email, payment details from Stripe Contract (Article 6(1)(b))

We do not sell your information. We do not use your CV or documents as samples, in marketing or in any other order. We do not use your information for any purpose other than those above.

6. How we write your documents

Every document we deliver is reviewed line by line by a person, who checks each claim against the information you gave us. We use document drafting and editing software from third-party providers as part of our writing process (see section 7).

Before your CV is entered into drafting software, we replace your name, email address, phone number, street address and similar identifiers with placeholders, and we put them back only in the final documents.

We do not make any decision about you based solely on automated processing that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

7. Who we share your information with

We share your information only with the service providers we need to run our business. They act as our processors: they may use it only on our instructions and must keep it secure.

Provider What they do for us Where the information is processed
Zoho (Zoho Corporation and group companies) Hosts our business email (hello@inkandoffer.com), including the CVs and documents sent by email United States
Document drafting and editing software providers (including AI-assisted writing tools) Help draft and edit the text of your documents, which a person then reviews line by line United States and/or European Union
GoatCounter (goatcounter.com) Counts website visits without cookies (see section 10) European Union
GitHub, Inc. Hosts our website United States

Stripe processes your payment. For payment processing, fraud prevention and its legal duties, Stripe acts as a separate controller under its own privacy policy: stripe.com/gb/privacy.

Google provides the fonts on our website. When your browser loads a page, it requests the fonts from Google's servers, and Google receives your IP address and browser details under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

We may also share information:

  • with our professional advisers (such as an accountant or solicitor), who are bound by confidentiality;
  • with HM Revenue & Customs, the police, a court or a regulator, where the law requires it; and
  • with a buyer or successor, if our business is ever sold or reorganised, under the same protections described here.

8. International transfers

Some of our providers process information outside the UK, including in the United States. When information leaves the UK, we make sure it is protected by one of the safeguards UK law allows: a UK "adequacy" decision for that country (including the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for US companies certified to it), or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Provider Safeguard
Zoho Zoho's data processing agreement with Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum, or the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where certified
Document drafting and editing software providers The UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, otherwise Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK Addendum
GitHub, Inc. The UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (GitHub is certified) and GitHub's data protection agreement
GoatCounter Not needed: the EU is covered by UK adequacy regulations

You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

9. How long we keep your information

Information How long we keep it
Your CV, the job advert, your instructions, our drafts and the final documents, and the emails and attachments that carried them 30 days after we deliver your final documents. If you ask for a revision, the 30 days run from when we deliver the revision. We then delete them from our email, storage and software accounts, including their bins or trash folders.
Any sensitive information you sent Deleted with your order files as above, or sooner if you ask
Business records: your name, email address, order reference, the service, the price and the payment date (no CV content) Up to 6 years after the end of the tax year, because tax law requires it
Records of your request to start early, any cancellation and any complaint (no CV content) For the same period as our business records, so that we can deal with any later question or claim
Marketing consent (if you give it) Until you withdraw it, plus a record that you withdrew it so that we don't contact you again

Deleted information may remain for a short time in our providers' backup systems, out of normal use, until it is overwritten: up to 90 days.

If you want your files deleted sooner, email us and we will delete them, except for anything we must keep by law. If we have not yet delivered, deleting your files will mean we cannot finish your order.

10. Our website, cookies and analytics

Our website does not use cookies or advertising tags. We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count page views. It sets no cookies, does not track you across sites, and does not store your full IP address; it records the page, the referring site, your browser type, screen size and approximate country, so we can see how the site is used. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in understanding which pages are useful.

  • Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, under its own privacy statement: docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. We do not have access to these logs.
  • Our website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads (see section 7).
  • If you click an email link on our website, your own email program opens. Nothing is sent to us until you send the email.

11. Keeping your information secure

We protect your information with measures appropriate to a small business handling CVs, including:

  • multi-factor authentication on our email, storage and software accounts;
  • encryption in transit and, through our providers, at rest;
  • access limited to the person working on your order;
  • devices with screen locks, disk encryption and current security updates; and
  • a weekly deletion routine to keep our 30-day commitment.

If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will report it to the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and we will tell you without undue delay if the risk to you is high.

12. Marketing

We will only send you marketing emails, such as news or offers, if you have opted in. You can withdraw your consent at any time by replying "unsubscribe" or emailing us, and we will stop. Emails about your order, such as confirmations, delivery and revision messages, are not marketing.

13. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your information;
  • object to our use of your information where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • data portability: receive the information you gave us, in a common electronic format, or have it sent to another organisation; and
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent. This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

Some rights apply only in certain circumstances. For example, we may need to keep business records for tax purposes even if you ask us to delete them.

To use any of these rights, email hello@inkandoffer.com. It is free. We may need to confirm your identity first. We will reply within one month, or tell you within that month if we need up to two more months because your request is complex.

If you live outside the UK, the privacy laws where you live may give you similar or additional rights. Contact us and we will help.

14. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so that we can try to put it right.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or 0303 123 1113. If you live outside the UK, you can also complain to your own data protection regulator, for example the Data Protection Commission in Ireland.

15. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice if the way we handle personal information changes. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If a change significantly affects an order you have already placed, we will tell you by email.
