CV and cover letter guides

By Ink & Offer · Updated October 2026

Practical, UK-focused advice for anyone applying for a job, especially if you haven't applied in a while, you're coming back after time away, or you're moving into a new field. Every example in these guides is fictional and labelled as such.

  • How to write a CV when you're over 50

    Lead with recent impact, summarise the early years and show current skills, without hiding anything.

  • How to write a CV after a career break

    One honest line for the gap, then a CV that points forward to the job you want now.

  • How to write a career change CV

    Answer the recruiter's three questions: why this move, why now, and can you do the job.

  • How to tailor your CV to a job advert, step by step

    An eight-step method, with a worked example of mapping an advert's requirements to real evidence.

  • How to write a cover letter that gets read

    Length, structure, UK sign-offs and a full fictional example letter.

  • How to write a finance or accounting CV

    Qualification status, systems, scope and numbers, tailored to the finance role you want.
